PART 1 — THE OFFER

Subject: Founding registration is open — Inventor Showcase, first publication catalog (granted patents only)

You hold a granted patent. Gate Nations LLC, a United States company, is opening the founding registration for its Inventor Showcase: a showcase built only for granted-patent inventions, published in a first catalog, with a serial-numbered, dated registry entry for every registered invention, and a closed room where identity- and capital-verified investors may request contact. Contact opens only when a verified investor asks — never the reverse.

Read this first: the platform is not built yet. What is open today is founding registration for the first publication catalog. The catalog is the first published edition of the showcase. Your founding place is a reserved, numbered place in that first catalog for one granted-patent invention. The first catalog will open on or before [DATE PENDING MAHMOUD'S DECISION: 1 March 2027]. If the first catalog does not open by that date, your payment is refunded in full.

What founding registration buys, exactly:

  1. Verification of your granted patent and your proof-of-function documents — these two checks, and nothing beyond them, are what "verified" means here.
  2. A showcase listing in the first catalog: a technical profile of the invention, your photos and video, under a platform identity. Your legal identity stays with us and is shown to a verified investor only at deal stage, inside the room.
  3. A serial-numbered, timestamped registry record of your listing.

Price, in the open — one payment, two paths, your choice:

  • Partner path — $15 total: a per-invention registration fee of $10 plus the first month of the Partner subscription at $5/month. The Partner path is cheaper because it carries a signed agreement, stated in full and in plain language in the disclosure below.
  • Full path — $110 total: the same registration fee of $10 plus one year of the Full subscription at $100/year. The Full path carries no percentage agreement, ever.

What we do NOT promise: no investor, license, sale, or income is promised or implied. Most registered inventions will not be commercialized. Please read the disclosure below before paying anything. If your invention's granted patent or proof-of-function cannot be verified, your payment is refunded in full and no listing is made.

Payment is made only through the company's own payment link, which accompanies this offer when registration opens for your cohort. We never ask for payment by any other route.

  • The Inventor Showcase is an invention showcase and introduction service operated by Gate Nations LLC. It is not a broker, agent, or guarantor of any transaction, and it is not built yet: founding registration reserves a place in its first publication catalog, which will open on or before [DATE PENDING MAHMOUD'S DECISION: 1 March 2027], or your payment is refunded in full.
  • Fees are for registration, verification, and showcase only, and are charged under those names. No fee buys an evaluation of commercial merit, and registration is not an endorsement of your invention's value or prospects.
  • Refunds, completely: (1) if the first catalog does not open by the date above — full refund; (2) if your granted patent or proof-of-function documents cannot be verified — full refund; (3) after your listing has been published in the catalog, the registration fee is not refundable, and a subscription may be cancelled at any time, stopping future charges.
  • Historical fact, stated plainly: invention promotion is a sector with a long record of inventor losses. Outcomes for individual inventions are rare and unpredictable. Anyone who promises you success is not telling the truth.
  • If you choose the Partner path, the agreement you sign before listing states, in plain language:

«The company's percentage (5%) is calculated on the gross amounts you actually receive from commercializing this one registered invention — before your own costs and taxes, and including non-cash consideration at the value stated in the transaction documents. The promise is fixed in the agreement you sign before listing, and it survives cancellation of your listing until the Term ends.»

PART 3 — AGREEMENT OPERATIVE TEXT

(the clause the package text stands on; verbatim from the Legal Chamber's final opinion, 21 October 2026)

  1. Registered Invention. The invention identified in your registration (Registration ID [___], by its title and, where applicable, its application or patent number), including any patent that continues from that same filing without new subject matter — continuations, divisionals, reissues, and foreign counterparts of that filing. Improvements and new inventions are not included.
  2. Receipts. Money and value you actually receive for the Registered Invention: license fees, royalties, option and milestone payments, advances, the price on a sale of the invention or its rights (including installments and earnouts as they are paid, and any part of a larger transaction's price that the transaction documents allocate to the invention), and amounts you recover for infringement of it. Amounts count when received; amounts a payer withholds for your taxes count as received when withheld. Non-cash consideration (such as shares) counts at the value stated in the transaction documents or, if none is stated, at fair market value; you may pay our percentage in cash or, where the consideration is divisible (such as shares), deliver the same percentage of it. If you transfer the invention to a related person or company for less than its value, the unpaid value is treated as a Receipt when transferred. Amounts you later repay reduce the base. Your own sales of products that use the invention are not Receipts, except for an allocated part of a transaction price as stated above.
  3. Payment and reporting. Five percent (5%) of Receipts, payable within thirty (30) days of each receipt, with a short statement identifying its source and amount. You will notify us within thirty (30) days of signing any license or sale of the Registered Invention and provide its payment terms. Once a year we may ask you to confirm your Receipts in writing.
  4. Term and survival. This promise runs for the Term stated in the agreement, from signing. Cancelling your subscription ends your listing only; this promise continues to the end of the Term. If we end your listing early without your breach, this promise ends when the listing ends.
  5. Transfers. You may sell or assign the Registered Invention freely. Unless the transferee assumes this promise in writing, you remain responsible for our percentage of everything you receive under that transfer.
  6. No securities engagement. This agreement does not engage the company to offer or sell securities or to raise capital for any person. If a transaction is a securities transaction, any fee on it requires a separate written arrangement through a registered broker-dealer.
  7. Law, language, signature. Mississippi law governs. The English text controls. An electronic signature binds the signer as a handwritten one. The signed agreement is kept with your registry entry, under its Registration ID and signing timestamp.
