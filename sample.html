Polybag Soil Rotor V2

A 4-in-1 soil tilling tool for fertigation farming — loosens, mixes, cuts roots, and opens planting holes inside polybags, without removing the soil.

THE INVENTION

The Polybag Soil Rotor V2 is a 4-in-1 soil tilling tool designed for fertigation farming. Fabricated from SK-5 steel and mild steel, the device features specialized rotor blades with a 22.5 mm width. It utilizes a dual-compatibility shaft that mounts to hand drills or lawn mowers. This innovation simultaneously performs soil loosening, mixing, root cutting, and creates planting holes directly within polybags without soil removal.

As described in the source catalogue, by optimizing soil structure it improves root growth and agricultural yields while reducing manual labor requirements. That sentence is the exhibitor's own catalogue description — it is reproduced here as their statement, not as a result verified by this platform.
