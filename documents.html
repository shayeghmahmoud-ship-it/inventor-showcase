Your documents

Send copies, never originals — your originals stay with you.

Custody. The documents you submit (your patent or application, proof of function, and similar materials) are stored under your Registration ID, exactly as received. We do not file, record, or submit any of your documents with any patent office or authority on your behalf, and sending us a document makes us neither your patent agent nor your attorney.

Access. Your documents are never posted publicly. They can be opened only by (1) our verification reviewers, so they can verify your listing, and (2) verified investors inside the closed room, and then only the documents you have released for investor viewing. Every opening is logged. We do not sell your documents, share them with anyone else, or use them — or anything they disclose about your invention — for any purpose except running this showcase: verifying, listing, and introducing. Receiving your documents gives the company no rights in your invention or in its disclosure.

Deletion and return. When your listing ends, when you cancel, or whenever you ask, we delete your submitted documents from active storage within 30 days and purge backup copies within a further 60 days. If your submission is not accepted for listing, the same deletion runs within 30 days of that decision. On request we confirm the deletion in writing. Two honest limits: the registration record and your signed agreement are kept as contract records for the agreement's Term and the legal record period, and deletion stops future access — it cannot recall a document an investor already lawfully viewed inside the closed room before the deletion.

If a court or authority legally compels us to produce a document, we will notify you first wherever the law allows.
